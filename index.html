BEHIND THE ARCHIVE

NOT A COMPANY.
JUST A FAN WITH A VISION.

ZenovX INDEPENDENT DEVELOPER

Hi, I'm ZenovX — an independent developer and fan of Satria Heroes and BIMA-X.

These projects grew from a desire to revisit classic games that deserve to be remembered. What began as curiosity became a personal journey of technical exploration, troubleshooting, restoration research, and community building.

I'm working independently, learning through experimentation, and documenting progress as I go. Every discovery, fix, and experiment is part of that journey.

This website is where I share the projects, explain what I'm working on, and connect with people who remember these games or want to see what happens next.

— ZenovX

THE REASON

SOME LEGACIES DESERVE ANOTHER CHANCE.

Satria Heroes and BIMA-X are part of the memories of their fans. As time passes, older games can become harder to access because of compatibility issues, missing resources, and outdated technical systems.

These projects explore ways to revisit those experiences, investigate technical problems, and keep the community connected to the games.

The goal is to learn, restore what is feasible, document discoveries, and celebrate the experiences that made these games memorable — while respecting the original creators and their intellectual property.

PROJECT 02 / DETAILED RECORD

BIMA-X

An independent fan project focused on restoring the classic BIMA-X experience and researching potential technical improvements.

Required game assets have been obtained and date compatibility is fixed. Character model graphics have been repaired to their intended quality, and online 1v1 multiplayer is now functional in initial testing across two separate devices.

MAJOR PROGRESS UPDATE

CHARACTER GRAPHICS FIXED.
ONLINE 1v1 NOW FUNCTIONAL.

Character graphics fixed. The issue that caused character models to appear pixelated, blurry, or unusually low-resolution has been resolved. Character visuals can now return to their intended quality. If the issue persists, clearing the game's cache may help.

Online 1v1 multiplayer implemented. Players can now connect using two separate devices and battle each other without requiring a direct Bluetooth connection. The feature passed initial testing with no crashes observed, though network lag is still present and further optimization is needed to improve stability and responsiveness.

EXPERIMENTAL BETA — FUNCTIONAL

THE NEXT BATTLE: ONLINE.

Online 1v1 multiplayer is now functional in initial testing. Players can connect using two separate devices and battle each other without requiring a direct Bluetooth connection — the original BIMA-X VS Friend mode relied on local Bluetooth, but this adaptation opens up internet-based play.

The feature has successfully passed initial testing with no crashes observed during those tests. However, network lag is still present, and further optimization is needed to improve stability and responsiveness.

This remains an experimental beta. The feasibility of the approach has been validated in practice, but public availability has not been confirmed.

HOW I WORK

CURIOSITY FIRST. PROMISES SECOND.

"This is an independent, evolving project."

Not every idea will become a working feature, and not every technical problem has an immediate solution.

Development updates will distinguish completed work from experiments and future plans. Transparency matters more than exaggerated promises. If a feature is still being researched, it will be presented as experimental. If a fix is completed, its status will be updated accordingly.
